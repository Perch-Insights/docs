Use this page to look up a word you see in Perch or in a Guide answer.

## Analyses and the Guide

- **Guide**: Perch's AI analyst. You ask it questions about your data in an analysis.
- **Analysis**: a conversation with the Guide about your data. The screen lists them under **Analyses**, and you start one with **New Analysis**.
- **Query**: a request for data that the Guide suggests in a **Query Suggestion** card. You decide whether to accept it.
- **Step**: an accepted query. Each accepted query becomes a step in the analysis.
- **Step number**: the order in which you built the analysis, such as **Step 1**. A sub-step sits one level below its step, such as step 4.2, and never deeper.
- **Computed metric**: a result the Guide calculates from your data. It is also called a derived metric.
- **Guide Question**: a card in which the Guide asks you to choose before it continues. You answer it or cancel it.

## Reports and playbooks

- **Insight report**: the finished write-up of an analysis, shown as an **Insight report** card. Also called a report.
- **Insight**: one section of a report, shown as its own card. You can copy it, vote on it, and pin it.
- **Finding**: a specific business observation from the data, written into an insight.
- **Pinned insight**: an insight you pinned. The Guide reads every pinned insight with each later question.
- **Playbook**: a saved set of repeatable analyses you can run again with fresh data.
- **Run**: one execution of a playbook. Each run is its own analysis with its own report.
- **Latest run report**: the report from a playbook's most recent run, shown on the playbook page.

## How Perch measures

- **Semantic layer**: the one shared set of metrics and dimensions that every analysis, dashboard, and Guide answer uses.
- **Metric**: a number Perch measures, such as contact rate. Each metric has one definition in the semantic layer.
- **Dimension**: a way to break a metric down, such as contact rate by region.
- **Journey**: a path from a start event to an outcome. Perch has four: lead, opportunity, interaction, and operational.
- **Operational lens**: a short-cycle comparison of now against prior comparable periods, focused on levers you can act on right away.
- **Cohort lens**: a longer-cycle comparison of groups of leads that share a start, measured at the same age.
- **Focus period**: the time window you are asking about right now.
- **Comparison period**: a time window the Guide measures the focus period against. An analysis can have more than one.
- **Baseline**: the program average that the Guide compares a segment against within the same period. It is not a comparison period.

## Monitoring

- **Dashboard**: a shared view of where your key numbers stand. The Perch team configures dashboards during onboarding.
- **Alert**: a notice that a metric crossed a threshold. It tells you where to look, not why.
- **Metrics catalog**: the list of metrics and dimensions in your workspace, with each one's description and type.

## Workspaces and people

- **Workspace**: the environment you work in, with its own dashboards, alerts, and analyses.
- **Member**: a person with access to a workspace.
- **Group**: a set of members. A group decides which dashboards its members can see.
