title: "Share an analysis"

After this page you will be able to switch an analysis between **Private** and **Public**, know what a viewer sees when they open your analysis, and clone any analysis you can open with **Clone Analysis**.

Every analysis you start is private. It appears only on your own analyses list, and only you can open it. A member who follows a link to it sees **Sorry, you do not have access to this analysis. Please contact the owner to grant you access.** with a **Return to Home** button. A public analysis appears on every member's analyses list. Any member can open it, but only as a viewer. You stay the owner.

## Make an analysis public or private

Only the owner sees this button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an analysis you own">
    In the header, next to the favorite star, find the button that reads **Private** with a lock icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Private">
    A dialog titled **Make Analysis public** opens and reads **All users with access to this workspace will be able to view this analysis**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**, or **Cancel** to leave the analysis private. The button turns green and reads **Public** with an open lock icon.

    <Frame caption="The confirmation dialog that opens when you click Private.">
      <img src="/images/share-an-analysis/click-confirm.png" alt="Dialog titled Make Analysis public with an information icon, the text All users with access to this workspace will be able to view this analysis, a purple Confirm button, and a Cancel button, over a blurred report" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To make the analysis private again, click **Public**. There is no confirmation dialog. The analysis disappears from other members' lists at once, and a member who opens a link to it sees the access message above.

## What a viewer can do

When you open an analysis that another member made public, you are a viewer. The header shows a **Viewer** badge, and a **Viewer Mode** box in the middle of the canvas reads **You may edit this analysis but no changes will be saved. If you wish to save your changes, first clone the thread to create your own copy.** **Dismiss** closes the box until you next open the analysis.

As a viewer you can:

- Read every step, and change the query and chart settings on a step to explore. Nothing you change is saved.
- Read the whole conversation with the Guide, including its reports and insights.
- Add the analysis to your favorites with the star in the header. Favorites are your own and do not affect the owner.
- Clone the analysis to get a copy you own.

As a viewer you cannot:

- Rename the analysis, or rename, duplicate, add, draft, or delete a step.
- Send a message to the Guide, stop it, answer a Guide Question, or accept a Query Suggestion. The message field is disabled.
- Change the **Private** or **Public** setting, or delete the analysis.

Those controls are not shown to a viewer.

## Clone an analysis

You can clone any analysis you can open, whether or not you own it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Clone Analysis">
    Do one of the following:

    - In an open analysis, click the menu button at the top right of the header and choose **Clone Analysis**.
    - On the analyses list, click **Clone Analysis** at the right end of the analysis's row.
    - As a viewer, click **Clone** in the **Viewer Mode** box.

    <Frame caption="The header menu of an open analysis, with Clone Analysis as its second entry.">
      <img src="/images/share-an-analysis/click-clone-analysis.png" alt="Top right of the analysis header showing the favorite star, a Private button with a lock icon, and an open menu button; the menu lists New Analysis, Clone Analysis, and Delete Analysis with icons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the copy">
    A dialog titled **Create a Copy of this Analysis** opens. The **Analysis Name** field is filled in with **Copy of** followed by the original name, and selected so you can type over it. The name can be up to 255 characters, cannot be only spaces, must differ from the original name, and must not match any other analysis in the workspace. Names are compared without regard to case.

    If you do not own the original, the dialog reminds you that your viewer changes will not carry over. The copy reflects the owner's last saved version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Clone">
    The copy opens. From the analyses list it opens in the current tab. From an open analysis it opens in a new browser tab, on the same step you were viewing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- The copy belongs to you, is private until you make it public, and has the same steps in the same order with their titles, queries, chart settings, and draft marks.
- The copy starts with a fresh conversation. The original's conversation with the Guide does not carry over.
- A playbook page has the same **Private** and **Public** button, shown only to its owner. See [Make a playbook public or private](/playbooks/save-a-playbook#make-a-playbook-public-or-private).
