After this page you will be able to switch an analysis between **Private** and **Public**, know what a viewer sees when they open your analysis, clone an analysis with **Clone Analysis** to make your own copy, and make a playbook public or private from its page.

## Who can see an analysis

Every analysis you start is private. A private analysis appears only on your own analyses list, and only you can open it. A member who follows a link to it sees **Sorry, you do not have access to this analysis. Please contact the owner to grant you access.** with a **Return to Home** button.

A public analysis appears on the analyses list of every member of the workspace. Any member can open it, but only as a viewer. You stay the owner, and your name stays in the **Owner** column.

## Make an analysis public or private

Only the owner of an analysis can change this setting. Other members do not see the button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an analysis you own">
    In the header at the top of the analysis, next to the favorite star, find the button that reads **Private** with a lock icon. Hovering over it shows **Make analysis public**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Private">
    A dialog titled **Make Analysis public** opens and reads **All users with access to this workspace will be able to view this analysis**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**, or **Cancel** to leave the analysis private. After you confirm, the message **Analysis status updated** appears and the button turns green and reads **Public** with an open lock icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To make the analysis private again, click **Public**. Hovering over it shows **Make analysis private**. There is no confirmation dialog this time. The button reads **Private** again and the message **Analysis status updated** appears.

<Note>
Making an analysis private takes effect at once. It disappears from other members' analyses lists, and a member who opens a link to it sees the access message above.
</Note>

## What a viewer can do

When you open an analysis that another member made public, you are a viewer. The header shows a **Viewer** badge with an info icon. Hovering over it explains that you can make changes during this session but they will not be saved, and that you can create a clone from the menu at the top right of the page to keep your own changes.

A **Viewer Mode** box also appears in the middle of the canvas with the same explanation and two buttons. **Clone** opens the clone dialog described below. **Dismiss** closes the box for this visit. It appears again the next time you open the analysis.

As a viewer you can:

- Read every step, and change the query and chart settings on a step to explore. Nothing you change is saved, and the owner and other viewers do not see your changes.
- Read the whole conversation with the Guide, including its reports and insights.
- Add the analysis to your favorites with the star in the header. Favorites are your own and do not affect the owner.
- Clone the analysis with **Clone Analysis** to get a copy you own.

As a viewer you cannot:

- Rename the analysis, rename a step, duplicate a step, add a step, mark a step as a draft, or delete a step. Those controls are not shown.
- Send a message to the Guide, stop it, answer a Guide Question, or accept a Query Suggestion. The Guide panel shows a notice that the Guide is disabled in viewer mode, and the message field is disabled. See [What you can do in an analysis you do not own](/analyses/ask-questions#what-you-can-do-in-an-analysis-you-do-not-own).
- Change the **Private** or **Public** setting, or delete the analysis. Those controls are not shown.

## Clone an analysis

Cloning creates a new analysis that you own, with the same steps as the original. You can clone any analysis you can open, whether or not you own it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Clone Analysis">
    Do one of the following:

    - In an open analysis, click the menu button at the top right of the header and choose **Clone Analysis**.
    - On the analyses list, click the **Clone Analysis** button at the right end of the analysis's row.
    - As a viewer, click **Clone** in the **Viewer Mode** box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the copy">
    A dialog titled **Create a Copy of this Analysis** opens. The **Analysis Name** field is filled in with **Copy of** followed by the original name, and the text is selected so you can type over it. The name can be up to 255 characters, cannot be only spaces, and must differ from the original name.

    If you do not own the original, the dialog also reminds you that any changes you made as a viewer will not carry over. The copy reflects the last version the owner saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Clone">
    The message **Analysis cloned successfully** appears and the copy opens. From the analyses list it opens in the current tab. From an open analysis it opens in a new browser tab, on the same step you were viewing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If another analysis in the workspace already has the name you chose, the message **Title must be unique** appears and nothing is created. Names are compared without regard to case. Any other problem shows **An unexpected error occurred**.

The copy:

- Belongs to you. The analyses list shows **You** as its owner.
- Is private until you make it public.
- Has the same steps in the same order, with their titles, queries, chart settings, and draft marks.
- Starts with a fresh conversation. The original's conversation with the Guide does not carry over.

## Make a playbook public or private

A playbook page has the same **Private** and **Public** button, shown only to the playbook's owner. A private playbook is visible only to you. A public playbook is visible to every member of the workspace. See [Make a playbook public or private](/playbooks/save-a-playbook#make-a-playbook-public-or-private).
