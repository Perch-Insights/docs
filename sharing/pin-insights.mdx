Pin the insights that matter so the Guide reads them with every later question and you can find them again with one click.

## Pin an insight

You can pin any insight in a finished report in an analysis you own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a finished report">
    In the **Guide** panel, expand the **Insight report** card. The pin button is not there while the header still reads **Writing…**. See [Read reports and insights](/analyses/reports-and-insights).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pin insight">
    Hover over the button at the right end of the insight's button row. It reads **Pin insight**. Click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the insight is pinned">
    The insight now carries a **Pinned** badge next to its type badge and a colored bar down its left edge, and the button reads **Unpin insight**. The report header shows **N pinned** under the title, and the **Pinned** button in the panel header shows the total across every report in the analysis.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From your next message, the Guide reads every pinned insight along with your question. You do not need to paste them. You can refer to them directly, for example "Consolidate my pinned insights".

## See only your pinned insights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Pinned">
    Click **Pinned** in the **Guide** panel header. The button turns solid and the heading **Pinned insights** appears above the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the pinned insights">
    The list holds only the reports with pinned insights. The first report is expanded and shows its summary followed by its pinned insights. The others start collapsed with their **N pinned** line. Click a header to expand or collapse a report. If nothing is pinned yet, the list reads **No pinned insights yet. Pin an insight from a report and it will show up here.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the full conversation">
    Click **Show full conversation** next to the heading, or click **Pinned** again. Sending a message, or answering or cancelling a **Guide Question**, also returns you to the full conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can copy, vote on, and unpin insights in this view exactly as in the full conversation. A report with no pinned insights left drops out of the list.

## Unpin an insight

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pinned insight">
    Open the report in the conversation, or click **Pinned** in the panel header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Unpin insight">
    Hover over the pin button at the right end of the insight's button row. It reads **Unpin insight**. Click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the insight is unpinned">
    The **Pinned** badge and the colored bar disappear, the **N pinned** line and the **Pinned** count go down by one, and the Guide stops reading that insight with your later messages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- An analysis can hold at most 30 pinned insights. If you try to pin another, the pin is removed again and an error message appears. Unpin an insight first, then try again.
- A pin stays in place until you unpin it, including after you close and reopen the analysis.
- Pins belong to the report they were made on. A finished report never changes, so if the Guide writes a new version of a report, the new report starts with no pins.
- In an analysis another member owns, you still see the owner's pins, the **N pinned** lines, and the **Pinned** filter, but there is no **Pin insight** or **Unpin insight** button. Only the owner can pin. See [What a viewer can do](/sharing/share-an-analysis#what-a-viewer-can-do).
