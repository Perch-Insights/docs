After this page you will know what your dashboards show, when to use one instead of an analysis, and that the Perch team sets them up for you during onboarding.

## What a dashboard is

A dashboard is a shared view of where your key numbers stand. It gives you high-level monitoring and the status of your key KPIs. Every member who can open a dashboard sees the same snapshot of performance.

Each dashboard belongs to a program. The **Dashboards** panel in the sidebar lists your **Favorites** first, then every other dashboard grouped by program name.

You see only the dashboards you have access to. Access is given to groups of members, so two members of one workspace can see different lists.

## Dashboards and analyses

A dashboard tells you what is happening. It is not the place for deep root-cause analysis. When a number moves and you need to know why, start an analysis and ask the Guide. An alert works the same way: it tells you something changed, and you look closer in a dashboard or an analysis.

Dashboards, analyses, and the Guide all use the same metric definitions, so a number means the same thing in each.

## How fresh the data is

When your workspace tracks data freshness, the line under a dashboard's name shows its program and **Data Refreshed as of:** with a date and time. That date belongs to the workspace's data, not to the one dashboard. Check it before you act on a number.

Some workspaces also have a data health status in the sidebar, shown as an icon with a green, yellow, or red dot. Hover it to see each **Business Domain** with its **Last Updated** time and **Status**. Click it to open the **Data Health Dashboard**.

## What you can and cannot change

The Perch team configures your dashboards during onboarding, against Perch standards, and changes them for you later. You do not build or edit a dashboard, and neither does the Guide.

What is yours to change:

- **Favorites.** Star a dashboard to keep it at the top of the panel. Your favorites do not change what other members see.
- **What you are looking at.** Filter the **Dashboards** screen by program or by name.

If you need access to a dashboard, ask a member who sees **Settings** in the sidebar. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
