---
title: "Open a dashboard"
description: "Find a dashboard in your workspace, open it, and check when its data was refreshed."
---

After this page you will be able to find a dashboard in your workspace, open it, mark it as a favorite, and see when its data was last refreshed.

The Perch team sets up your dashboards during onboarding. There is no button for you to create one. If a dashboard you need is missing, see [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).

## Open a dashboard from the sidebar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboards panel">
    Click the dashboards icon in the sidebar. Its tooltip reads **Dashboards**. A panel titled **Dashboards** opens next to the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the dashboard">
    The panel lists your **Favorites** first. Below them, every other dashboard is grouped under its program name. Program groups start collapsed, except the one holding the dashboard you have open. Click a program name to expand or collapse its group. To find a dashboard by name, type in the **Search...** field at the top of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click the dashboard's name. The panel closes and the dashboard opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browse every dashboard

The **Dashboards** screen shows all your dashboards in one table. Use it when you want to filter by program.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboards screen">
    In the **Dashboards** panel, click the **Dashboards** title at the top. The screen's header reads **Dashboards**, with a count such as **Showing 12 dashboards** under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Click **Programs** and tick one or more programs to show only their dashboards. To find a dashboard by name, type in the **Search...** field. Clear a program filter with the cross on the **Programs** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a dashboard">
    The table has a **Name** column and a **Program** column. Click a dashboard's name to open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the dashboard header

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the name and program">
    The header shows the dashboard's name. The line under it shows the program the dashboard belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check how fresh the data is">
    When your workspace tracks data freshness, the same line ends with **Data Refreshed as of:** and a date and time, such as **September 29, 2026 9:15AM**. The date is the same on every dashboard in the workspace. If you do not see it, your workspace does not track data freshness.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the dashboard">
    The dashboard itself fills the page below the header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark a dashboard as a favorite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the star">
    Click the star at the right of the dashboard header. Its tooltip reads **Add to Favorites**. In the table on the **Dashboards** screen, the same star sits before each dashboard's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    The star turns yellow and Perch shows **Dashboard status updated**. The dashboard now appears under **Favorites** in the **Dashboards** panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a favorite, click the yellow star again. Its tooltip reads **Remove from Favorites**.

## Good to know

- You see only the dashboards you have access to. If a dashboard a colleague mentions is not in your list, ask a member who sees **Settings** in the sidebar. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
- A dashboard link you cannot open shows **Dashboard not found** and **Nothing to see**.
- Favorites are yours. Starring a dashboard does not change what other members see.
- A dashboard shows what is happening, not why. When a number needs explaining, [start an analysis](/analyses/start-an-analysis) and ask the Guide.
