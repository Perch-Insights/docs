---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What Perch is and how the Guide helps you get answers from your data."
---

Perch is where you go to get answers from your organization's data. You sign in, open a workspace, and see every saved analysis in that workspace in one list, next to the playbooks you can run again.

The Guide is Perch's AI analyst. Open an analysis, type a question about your data, and the Guide suggests queries, builds steps on the canvas, and writes insight reports you can copy, download, pin, and share. When a line of analysis is worth repeating, the Guide can save it as a playbook so you can run it again for a fresh report.
