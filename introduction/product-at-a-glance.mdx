Perch has seven areas. This page tells you what each one is, who uses it, and when to reach for it. The sidebar on the left of every screen opens most of them.

## Analyses and the Guide

An analysis is a conversation with the Guide about your data. You ask a question, the Guide suggests queries, and each query you accept becomes a step. This is where you work out why a number moved. Any member can start one. The **Analyses** screen lists every saved analysis in your workspace. Read [Analyses and the Guide](/analyses/overview).

## Playbooks

A playbook is a reusable analysis. The Guide offers to save a line of analysis as a playbook, so you can run it again on fresh data. Each run is its own analysis with its own report. The playbook page shows the latest one as its **Latest run report**. Reach for a playbook when you need the same analysis again. Playbooks sit next to your analyses on the **Analyses** screen. Read [Playbooks](/playbooks/overview).

## Reports and sharing

When the Guide finishes, it writes an **Insight report**. Each section of the report is an insight, shown as a card. You can copy or download the report and pin the insights that matter. Every analysis you start is private. When you make it public, every member of the workspace can open it as a viewer and clone it to build on it. Read [Reports and sharing](/sharing/overview).

## Dashboards

Dashboards show where your key numbers stand. They give everyone in the workspace the same snapshot of performance, and each one shows when its data was last refreshed. Use a dashboard to see what is happening. Use an analysis to find out why. The Perch team configures your dashboards during onboarding, against Perch standards. You open and read them from **Dashboards** in the sidebar. Read [Dashboards](/dashboards/overview).

## Alerts

An alert tells you that an important change happened, because a metric crossed a threshold. The **Alerts** screen lists your alert notifications. An alert is a signal, not an answer. It tells you where to look, and you investigate in a dashboard or an analysis. The Perch team configures your alerts during onboarding. Read [Alerts](/alerts/overview).

## Metrics catalog

The **Metrics Catalog** lists the metrics and dimensions in your workspace, each with its name, description, and type. These are the same definitions the Guide, your analyses, and your dashboards use. Reach for it when you want to know exactly what a metric measures. The metrics catalog is not turned on in every workspace, so your sidebar may not show it. Read [Metrics catalog](/metrics-catalog/overview).

## Administration

Members who manage people or workspaces see a **Settings** button in the sidebar. It opens the **Organization** settings, with **Users**, **Groups**, and **Workspaces**. Most members never see it. Metric definitions, dashboards, and alerts are not changed here. The Perch team changes them for you. Read [Administration](/administration/overview).

## When you need the Perch team

The **Support** button at the bottom of the sidebar opens a form where you describe the problem you are facing. Use it for anything you cannot change yourself. [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services) lists which changes are yours and which belong to the Perch team.
