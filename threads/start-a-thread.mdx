---
title: "Start a thread"
description: "Open a new thread from the analyses list and find the ones you already have."
---

After this page you will be able to open the **Analyses** list for your workspace, click **New Analysis** to start a thread with the Guide, rename the thread, mark it as a favorite, and find an existing thread by name, owner, or favorite status.
