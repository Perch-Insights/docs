---
title: "Save a playbook"
description: "Save an analysis as a playbook you can find on the analyses list, read, rename, share, and change with the Guide."
---

After this page you will be able to reply to a **Playbook proposal** in the chat to save it, open the playbook from the **Playbooks** section of the analyses list, read its description and **Plan**, rename it, make it **Public** or **Private**, and start an edit analysis with the Guide to change it.

## Save a playbook from an analysis

A playbook is a reusable analysis the Guide can run again. The Guide saves one only after it has proposed it and you have agreed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the Guide to save the analysis">
    In an analysis you own, type a request such as **Save this as a playbook** in the **Guide** panel and send it. The Guide answers with a **Playbook proposal** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the proposal">
    The card header shows the Guide icon, the label **Playbook proposal**, the proposed name, and a grey **Pending** badge. The body below describes what the playbook will do. Click the header to collapse or expand the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply to the Guide">
    The bottom of the card tells you how to answer: reply **save this playbook** to keep it, **discard this** to dismiss it, or describe what you would like changed. Type your reply in the **Ask about your data...** field as you would any other message. The Guide saves the playbook only when your reply clearly says to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Once the playbook is saved, the badge turns green and reads **Accepted**, the card collapses, and the name in the card header becomes a link that opens the playbook in a new browser tab. If you discard the proposal, the badge turns red and reads **Rejected** with an X, and the card collapses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the playbook is saved, the line under the analysis title reads **in** followed by the playbook name. Click that name to open the playbook. When the Guide mentions the playbook by name in a reply, that name is also a link that opens the playbook in a new tab.

If you ask the Guide to change an analysis that already belongs to a playbook, or you name an existing playbook, the Guide proposes an update instead. The name in the card header links to the playbook, and the note at the bottom offers **update** followed by the playbook name to apply the changes, **save as a new playbook** to create a separate one, or **discard this**.

<Note>
The reply instructions appear only in an analysis you own. If the playbook you name belongs to another member and is private, the Guide proposes a new playbook of your own instead of an update to theirs.
</Note>

## Find your playbooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the analyses list">
    Click **Analyses** in the left sidebar, or the home button at the top left of any analysis. Once the workspace has at least one playbook you can see, a **Playbooks** section appears above the **Analyses** section with the subtitle **Reusable analyses you can re-run.** and a count in the form **Showing 6 of 9 playbooks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the playbook cards">
    Each card shows the playbook name, the first two lines of its description, and an owner badge that reads **You** for your own playbooks or the owner's name for anyone else's. A playbook that has been run also shows a **Last run** link with how long ago it ran. Click that link to open the analysis of the latest run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through the list">
    The section shows six playbooks at a time. Use the **Previous playbooks** and **Next playbooks** arrow buttons on either side to move between pages. Playbooks that ran most recently come first. Playbooks that have never run come last.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a playbook">
    Click anywhere on a card to open the playbook page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see every public playbook in the workspace plus your own private ones. Other members' private playbooks do not appear.

In the **Analyses** section below, the **Playbook** column shows a chip with the playbook name on every analysis that saved or ran a playbook. Click the chip to open that playbook.

## Read a playbook page

The header of a playbook page shows the playbook name, then **Created at** with the date and time and **by** with the owner badge. On the right are the buttons **Plan** and **Run**. If you own the playbook, a **Private** or **Public** button and an **Edit** button appear as well.

Below the header:

- **Description** is the Guide's summary of what the playbook does.
- **Latest run report** and the **Past Runs** list show what the playbook produced when it ran. See [Run a playbook](/playbooks/run-a-playbook).

### Read the plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    Click **Plan** in the header. The **Analysis plan** dialog opens with the full plan the Guide follows on every run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close it">
    Click the X close button in the dialog to return to the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you own the playbook, the bottom of the dialog reads **Edit the plan by chatting with the Guide** next to an **Edit** button. See [Change a playbook with the Guide](#change-a-playbook-with-the-guide).

If the playbook no longer exists, the page reads **Playbook not found**. If it is another member's private playbook, the page reads **Sorry, you do not have access to this playbook. Please contact the owner to grant you access.** with a **Return to Home** button that takes you back to the analyses list.

## Rename a playbook

You can rename a playbook you own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the name">
    Click the playbook name in the header. It becomes an editable field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name">
    The field accepts up to 255 characters and shows a counter such as **240/255** as you approach the limit. Perch saves names of at most 200 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press Enter or click anywhere outside the field. Perch confirms with **Playbook renamed**. Extra spaces are trimmed, and if you did not change the name nothing happens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new name appears at once in the **in** badge of every analysis that belongs to the playbook and on its card in the **Playbooks** section.

Two names are rejected:

- An empty name. Perch shows **Title cannot be empty** and keeps the old name.
- A name another playbook in the workspace already uses, in any letter case. Perch shows **A playbook with this name already exists** and keeps the old name.

A name longer than 200 characters fails with **Failed to rename playbook**.

## Make a playbook public or private

The button in the header shows the playbook's current visibility: **Private** with a lock icon, or **Public** with an open lock. Only the owner sees this button. A private playbook is visible only to you. A public playbook is visible to every member of the workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the visibility button">
    Hover over it to see **Make playbook public** or **Make playbook private**, then click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm when making it public">
    Making a playbook public opens a dialog titled **Make Playbook public** that reads **All users with access to this workspace will be able to view this playbook**. Click **Confirm**. Making it private again needs no confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Perch confirms with **Playbook visibility updated** and the button switches label and icon. If the change fails, Perch shows **Failed to update playbook visibility** and the button returns to its previous state.

A playbook the Guide saves is private unless you asked the Guide to share it with the workspace when it was proposed.

## Change a playbook with the Guide

You cannot edit the plan directly. Instead, you open an analysis that is tied to the playbook and ask the Guide for the change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    On a playbook you own, click **Edit** in the header, or **Edit** at the bottom of the **Analysis plan** dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the edit analysis">
    Perch creates a new analysis named with today's date followed by **Playbook Edit**, such as **July 3, 2024 • Playbook Edit**, and opens it. Its header shows **in** and the playbook name. Perch does not run the playbook when it opens this analysis. If the analysis cannot be created, Perch shows **Failed to start editing playbook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Tell the Guide what to change in the **Guide** panel. The Guide answers with a **Playbook proposal** card whose header links to the playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard the change">
    Reply **update** followed by the playbook name to apply the change, **save as a new playbook** to keep the original and create a new one, or **discard this**. The badge on the card turns to **Accepted** or **Rejected** as described above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See a playbook's details in the chat

When you ask the Guide to show the full details of a playbook it has mentioned, it posts a **Playbook** card. The card header shows the Guide icon, the label **Playbook**, and the playbook name as a link that opens the playbook in a new tab. The body holds the playbook's full details. Click the header to collapse or expand the card. This card is read-only and has nothing to accept or reject.

## What you can do with a playbook you do not own

On a public playbook that another member owns, you can open the page, read the **Description**, open the **Plan**, run it, and open its runs. There is no **Edit** button, no visibility button, the **Analysis plan** dialog has no **Edit** footer, and clicking the name does not open the rename field.
