---

After this page you can save an analysis as a playbook, find it on the analyses list, read its **Plan**, rename it, make it **Public** or **Private**, and change it with the Guide.

## Save a playbook from an analysis

A playbook is a reusable analysis the Guide can run again. The Guide saves one only after it has proposed it and you have agreed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the Guide to save the analysis">
    In an analysis you own, send a request such as **Save this as a playbook**. The Guide answers with a **Playbook proposal** card showing the proposed name and what the playbook will do.

    <Frame caption="The Playbook proposal card while the proposal is still pending.">
      <img src="/images/save-a-playbook/ask-the-guide-to-save-the-analysis.png" alt="Card headed Playbook proposal with the proposed name Inbound Call Volume by Day of Week, a Pending badge, and the opening of the description with the plan's primary metric and methodology steps." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply to the Guide">
    Reply **save this playbook** to keep it, **discard this** to dismiss it, or describe what you would like changed. The Guide saves the playbook only when your reply clearly says to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the playbook">
    Once saved, the badge on the card reads **Accepted** and the name in the card header links to the playbook. The line under the analysis title now reads **in** and the playbook name, also a link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the analysis already belongs to a playbook, or you name an existing playbook, the Guide proposes an update instead. See [Change a playbook with the Guide](#change-a-playbook-with-the-guide) for the replies.

## Find your playbooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the analyses list">
    Click **Analyses** in the left sidebar. Once the workspace has at least one playbook you can see, a **Playbooks** section appears above the **Analyses** section.

    <Frame caption="The Playbooks section sitting above the Analyses section on the analyses list.">
      <img src="/images/save-a-playbook/open-the-analyses-list.png" alt="Playbooks section reading Showing 1 of 1 playbooks with one card titled Docs: Inbound call volume playbook, a You badge, and a Last run link, above the Analyses section heading and the New Analysis button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the cards">
    Each card shows the playbook name, the start of its description, and an owner badge that reads **You** for your own. A playbook that has run also shows a **Last run** link to the analysis of its latest run. Playbooks that ran most recently come first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a playbook">
    The section shows six playbooks at a time. Use the **Previous playbooks** and **Next playbooks** arrows to page through, then click a card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see every public playbook in the workspace plus your own private ones.

## Read a playbook page

The header shows the playbook name, **Created at** with the date, and **by** with the owner. On the right are the **Plan** and **Run** buttons. Owners also see a **Private** or **Public** button and an **Edit** button.

**Description** below the header is the Guide's summary of the playbook. **Latest run report** and the **Past Runs** list show what it produced. See [Run a playbook](/playbooks/run-a-playbook).

Click **Plan** to open the **Analysis plan** dialog with the plan the Guide follows on every run. For owners, the bottom of the dialog reads **Edit the plan by chatting with the Guide** next to an **Edit** button.

## Rename a playbook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the name">
    On a playbook you own, click the name in the header to make it editable.

    <Frame caption="The playbook name in the page header.">
      <img src="/images/save-a-playbook/click-the-name.png" alt="Playbook page header with the name Docs: Inbound call volume playbook, followed by Created at with a date and by with a You badge." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name and save">
    Press Enter or click anywhere outside the field. The new name appears at once on every analysis that belongs to the playbook and on its card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Perch rejects an empty name, a name longer than 200 characters, and a name another playbook in the workspace already uses in any letter case.

## Make a playbook public or private

A private playbook is visible only to you. A public playbook is visible to every member of the workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the visibility button">
    On a playbook you own, click **Private** or **Public** in the header.

    <Frame caption="The owner's buttons on the right of the playbook header.">
      <img src="/images/save-a-playbook/click-the-visibility-button.png" alt="Four buttons in a row: Private with a padlock icon, Plan, Edit, and a purple Run button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm when making it public">
    Making a playbook public opens a dialog titled **Make Playbook public**. Click **Confirm**. Making it private again needs no confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a playbook with the Guide

You cannot edit the plan directly. Ask the Guide instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    On a playbook you own, click **Edit** in the header or in the **Analysis plan** dialog. Perch creates a new analysis named with today's date followed by **Playbook Edit** and opens it without running the playbook.

    <Frame caption="The header of the new analysis Perch opens for the edit.">
      <img src="/images/save-a-playbook/click-edit.png" alt="Analysis header titled September 25, 2026 Playbook Edit, with Created at and a date, the word in followed by a link to Docs: Inbound call volume playbook, and by with a You badge." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Tell the Guide what to change. The Guide answers with a **Playbook proposal** card whose header links to the playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard the change">
    Reply **update** followed by the playbook name to apply the change, **save as a new playbook** to leave the original untouched, or **discard this**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- A playbook the Guide saves is private unless you asked the Guide to share it with the workspace when it was proposed.
- On a public playbook another member owns, you can read the **Description**, open the **Plan**, run it, and open its runs. You cannot rename it, and the **Edit** and visibility buttons do not appear.
