After this page you will know what the metrics catalog lists and why the metrics and dimensions in it are the ones the Guide uses.

## What the metrics catalog is

The **Metrics Catalog** is the list of every metric and dimension in your workspace's semantic layer. A metric is a number Perch measures. A dimension is a way to break a metric down, such as by region. The screen calls them fields and shows how many there are at the top.

Each row in the list shows:

- **Name**: the field's readable name, with its technical name underneath.
- **Description**: what the field means.
- **Type**: **Measure** for a metric, **Dimension** for a dimension.

Open a field to see its own page. It shows the full **Description** and, where the field is calculated, a **Formula** with any **Filters** it applies. Fields named inside a formula link to their own pages. A field's page can also list **Related Metrics** and **Dimensions**, each linking to its page, so you can move from one definition to the next.

## Why every metric has one definition

Perch keeps one shared set of metrics and dimensions, each with a clear, fixed definition. The catalog shows you those definitions. It does not hold a copy of them.

That matters because everything else in Perch uses the same set:

- **The Guide** answers from these metrics and dimensions. It does not invent a definition or a calculation.
- **Analyses** are built from them, step by step.
- **Dashboards** and **alerts** report the same metrics.

So when a number looks surprising in a dashboard, an alert, or a Guide answer, the catalog tells you exactly what that number measures. Two people looking at the same metric are always looking at the same calculation.

## What you can and cannot change

The catalog is read-only. You cannot add, edit, or remove a metric or dimension there, and the Guide cannot change a definition either. The Perch team maintains the semantic layer and evolves it with you. Some changes are kept out of self-service on purpose, so your numbers stay consistent and comparable across time and teams. If a definition looks wrong, or you need a metric that is not listed, ask the Perch team. See [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).

What is yours is your view. You can narrow the list by type or search it by name. That changes only what you see.

The metrics catalog is not turned on in every workspace. If your sidebar has no **Metric Catalog** icon, it is not available to you yet.
