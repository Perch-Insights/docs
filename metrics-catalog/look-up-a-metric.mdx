---
title: "Look up a metric"
description: "Search the metrics catalog and open a metric or dimension to read its details."
---

After this page you will be able to search the metrics catalog by name or type and open a field to read its details.

The catalog is read-only. You can look up any metric or dimension, but you cannot change one. To learn what the catalog is for, see [Metrics catalog](/metrics-catalog/overview).

## Find a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Metrics Catalog">
    Click the metrics catalog icon in the sidebar. Its tooltip reads **Metric Catalog**. The screen's header reads **Metrics Catalog**, with a count such as **Showing 240 fields** under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the list">
    Each row shows the field's **Name**, its **Description**, and its **Type**. The name is in bold, with the technical name underneath. The type is **Measure** for a metric and **Dimension** for a dimension. Without a search, the list is in alphabetical order by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by type">
    Click **Types** and tick **Measure**, **Dimension**, or both. The button then shows the types you picked. Clear the filter with the cross on the button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by name">
    Type in the **Search...** field. The search matches names loosely, so a near spelling still finds the field. The closest matches come first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between pages">
    The list shows 19 fields per page. Use the arrows next to **Page 1 of 13** to go to the next or previous page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field">
    Click a field's name in the list. Its page opens. The header shows **Metrics Catalog**, an arrow, and the field's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the description and formula">
    **Description** tells you what the field means. If the field is calculated, a **Formula** box shows how. Any **Filters** the calculation applies are listed under it. A field named inside the formula is shown as a grey chip. Click the chip to open that field's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow related fields">
    Below the formula, a metric can list **Related Metrics** and **Dimensions**. Each entry is a card marked **Measure** or **Dimension**. Click a card to open that field. When you open a dimension from a metric's list, the header shows the metric between **Metrics Catalog** and the dimension, so you can click back to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the list">
    Click **Metrics Catalog** in the header, or click the cross at the top right of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The right side of a field page lists the field's technical properties. You do not need them to read the definition.

## Good to know

- Filters and searches change only your view. They do not change the catalog.
- The Guide answers from the same metrics and dimensions you see here. When a number in an answer, a dashboard, or an alert looks surprising, look up the metric to see exactly what it measures.
- If a definition looks wrong, or a metric you need is missing, ask the Perch team. See [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).
- The metrics catalog is not turned on in every workspace. If your sidebar has no **Metric Catalog** icon, it is not available to you yet.
