---
title: "Administer a workspace"
description: "The path for members who manage who can use Perch and what they can reach."
---

Follow this path to open the organization settings and manage the members, groups, and workspaces you are allowed to change.

This path is for the few members who look after access for everyone else. You decide who can sign in, how members are grouped, and which dashboards each group sees. The settings cover your whole organization, not a single workspace. What the numbers mean and how dashboards and alerts are built stays with the Perch team. Each step below links to the page that covers it.

1. **Sign in and choose a workspace.** Sign in like any other member. See [Sign in and choose a workspace](/getting-started/sign-in).
2. **Open the organization settings.** Click **Settings** in the sidebar. You only see it if you are allowed to manage members or workspaces. It opens the **Organization** panel with **Users** and **Groups**. If you can also create, update, or delete workspaces, you see **Workspaces** as well. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
3. **Review who has access.** **Users** lists every member of your organization and each member's groups. Narrow the list by **Groups** or by name. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
4. **Add or change a member.** Click **Add user** and enter the member's **Email**, **First name**, and **Last name**. You can place them in groups straight away with **Groups (optional)**. Each member has **Update** and **Delete** actions, and **Resend Invite** while they have not confirmed their account. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
5. **Organize members into groups.** On **Groups**, click **Add Group** and give it a name. Open a group to see its **Members** tab. Click **Add member** to **Select users** who already exist or to **Add new user**, and click **Remove** to take a member out. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
6. **Choose the dashboards each group can see.** A group's **Dashboards** tab lists every dashboard with its program. Turn the switch on a dashboard to add it to the group or remove it. The Perch team builds the dashboards themselves during onboarding. See [Dashboards](/dashboards/overview).
7. **Ask the Perch team for everything else.** Metric definitions, dashboards, and alerts are not changed in the organization settings. Click **Support** in the sidebar to ask for a change. See [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).

To use Perch yourself, follow [Analyze with the Guide](/getting-started/analyze-with-the-guide) or [View and monitor](/getting-started/view-and-monitor).
