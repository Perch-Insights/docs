title: "Analyze with the Guide"
description: "The path from your first question to a report you can share and a playbook you can run again."
---

Follow this path to go from your first question to the Guide, through a finished report, to a playbook you can run again.

Something changed in your numbers and you want to know why. In Perch you work that out in an analysis, a conversation with the Guide. You ask, the Guide suggests queries, and each query you accept becomes a step. The Guide then writes up what it found as a report. Each step below links to the page that shows you how.

1. **Sign in and choose a workspace.** Perch opens the analyses list of your first workspace. Switch to another workspace if your data lives there. See [Sign in and choose a workspace](/getting-started/sign-in).
2. **Start an analysis.** Start from **New Analysis** on the analyses list. The analysis opens with the canvas in the middle and the **Guide** panel on the right. See [Start an analysis](/analyses/start-an-analysis).
3. **Ask your question and build the steps.** Ask the Guide in plain words, by typing or by speaking. The Guide may ask a few questions of its own first. It proposes each query as a **Query Suggestion**. Each one you accept becomes a step on the canvas. See [Ask the Guide a question](/analyses/ask-questions).
4. **Read the insight report.** The Guide writes up its findings as an **Insight report** in the **Guide** panel. Each insight is its own card. You can copy the report, vote on it, or download it as a file. See [Read reports and insights](/analyses/reports-and-insights).
5. **Pin the insights that matter.** A pinned insight stays in the Guide's mind for every later question, and you can find it again with one click. See [Pin insights](/sharing/pin-insights).
6. **Share the analysis.** Every analysis you start is private. Make it public, and every member of the workspace can open it as a viewer and clone it. See [Share an analysis](/sharing/share-an-analysis).
7. **Save it as a playbook and run it again.** When you will need the same analysis again, ask the Guide to save it as a playbook. Each **Run** of the playbook creates a new analysis with its own report. See [Save a playbook](/playbooks/save-a-playbook) and [Run a playbook](/playbooks/run-a-playbook).

To see how analyses, steps, and the Guide fit together, read [Analyses and the Guide](/analyses/overview). If you mostly watch your numbers rather than investigate them, follow [View and monitor](/getting-started/view-and-monitor) instead.
