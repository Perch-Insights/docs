---
title: "Sign in and choose a workspace"
description: "Sign in to Perch, land in your first workspace, and switch to another one."
---

After this page you will be able to sign in with your email and password, land on the analyses list of your first workspace, and open the **Workspaces** panel to switch to another workspace you are a member of.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Open Perch in your browser. If you are not signed in, you see a page with the heading **Login**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your email address in the **Email** field and your password in the **Password** field.

    <Frame caption="The login form with the Email field focused.">
      <img src="/images/sign-in/enter-your-credentials.png" alt="Card headed Login with an empty Email field, an empty Password field, a Forgot your password? link, and a Login button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Login">
    Click **Login**. Perch opens the analyses list of your first workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your first workspace is the one whose name comes first alphabetically among the workspaces you are a member of. The analyses list has an **Analyses** section that shows every saved analysis in that workspace. If the workspace has any playbooks, a **Playbooks** section appears above it.

If you are already signed in and open the login page again, Perch sends you straight to your workspace.

<Note>
If you are not a member of any workspace yet, Perch shows a page that says **Nothing to see here**. You need to be added to a workspace before you can start an analysis.
</Note>

<Note>
If your workspace is not set up for the Guide, the analyses list is replaced by the message **This Workspace does not have AI permissions**.
</Note>

## Switch to another workspace

The left sidebar shows a workspace button directly under the Perch logo. It shows the name of the workspace you are in. Hover over it to see the full name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workspaces panel">
    Click the workspace button in the left sidebar. A panel titled **Workspaces** opens next to it and lists every workspace you are a member of, in alphabetical order. The workspace you are in is highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace">
    Click a workspace name. The panel closes and Perch opens that workspace's analyses list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Analyses and playbooks belong to one workspace. When you switch workspaces, the list changes to show only what is saved in the workspace you picked.

## Sign out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account page">
    Click **Account** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Log out">
    In the **Personal** section of the account panel, click **Log out**. Perch confirms with the message **Logged out successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
