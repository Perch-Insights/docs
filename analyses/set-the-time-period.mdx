title: "Set the time period"

On this page you learn to set the dates a step covers and how its data is grouped over time.

Two buttons sit after **Filters** in the row below the step title. The first shows the granularity, such as **Monthly**. The second shows the time period, such as `05/01/26 to 07/31/26`. As with the other pickers, your changes wait until you click **Update Analysis**. See [Build a step](/analyses/build-a-step).

## Choose the granularity

The granularity sets how the data is grouped: one row or point per day, week, month, or year.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click the granularity button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a grouping">
    Click **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly**. Click **All** to get one total for each period you select instead of a breakdown over time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the menu, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pick a date range

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the time period">
    Click the time period button, then click **Range**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the dates">
    Click a start date and an end date on the calendar. You cannot pick a date after today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more ranges">
    Click **Add Range** to add another range, up to ten. Click a range in the list to change it, or its X to remove it. With more than one range, the button reads **Date Ranges**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the time period, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pick whole periods

Use **Period** to pick whole days, weeks, months, or years, such as April, May, and June.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to periods">
    Open the time period and click **Period**. The button then reads **Time Period**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the unit">
    Click the unit above the calendar, such as **Monthly**, and pick **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly**. Changing the unit clears your picks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick periods">
    Click each period you want. Click a picked period again to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the time period, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare a period with earlier ones

Use **Compare** to set a current period and the periods to compare it with. A step set to **Last Week** moves forward on its own each week. You do not edit the dates again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to compare">
    Open the time period and click **Compare**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the unit">
    Under **Granularity**, click **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**. The step's granularity changes to match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the current period">
    Under **Select a period to analyze**, pick the current or last one, such as **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, or **Last Month**. Or pick the custom option, such as **Custom Week**, and choose a date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to compare with">
    Under **Select a period to compare**, tick the box before **prior weeks** (or days, months, years) and choose how many, such as 4. With **Day**, you can also compare with the same weekday a number of weeks back. With **Month**, you can compare with the same month a number of years back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the time period, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep only some days

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filters">
    In the time period, click **Advanced Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose days">
    Under **Days of Week**, click **Weekdays** or **Weekends**, or click single days. Under **Days of Month**, click days, or set **Select days** from one day to another and click **Apply**. With **Yearly** or **All**, **Months of Year** lets you keep only some months.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the time period, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Apply to every step

Tick **Apply these dates to all steps** in the time period, or **Apply this granularity to all steps** in the granularity menu. Click **Update Analysis**. Every step in the analysis takes the new setting. You cannot leave a step out. Applying dates keeps each step's own granularity.

## Good to know

- With **All**, each range, period, or comparison becomes one column with its total. Last week compared with the 4 prior weeks gives two columns. Three ranges give three columns.
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
- If no data exists for the dates you pick, the step says **Unfortunately, we couldn't find any data for this request.** Change the dates and update again.
- If you are not the owner of the analysis, your changes apply to your view only and are not saved.
