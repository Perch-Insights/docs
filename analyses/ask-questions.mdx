---
title: "Ask the Guide a question"
description: "Type a question in the Guide panel, answer its follow-ups, and turn its query suggestions into steps."
---

After this page you will be able to type or dictate a question in the **Guide** panel, stop the Guide while it works, answer a **Guide Question** when it needs more detail, and accept, reject, or undo a **Query Suggestion** so it becomes a step on the canvas.

## Ask a question

The **Guide** panel is on the right of every analysis. Before you ask anything, the conversation reads **Let's start analyzing your data.** and the cursor is already in the field at the bottom, which reads **Ask about your data...**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Type in the field at the bottom of the panel. Press **Shift+Enter** to add a line break.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button at the right end of the field. The button stays disabled until the field contains some text. Your question appears on the right side of the conversation and the field clears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the answer">
    While the Guide works, a card with three moving dots and a short status such as **Pulling the numbers...** appears at the bottom of the conversation. Above it, a grey pill names what the Guide is doing right now. The Guide's answer appears on the left side of the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Guide takes the step you currently have open on the canvas as the context for your question. Select a different step before you send if you want to ask about that one instead.

You cannot send another message while the Guide is working. The field is disabled until the answer is complete.

Long questions are cut off after a few lines. Click **Show more** under the message to read the whole thing, and **Show less** to fold it again.

Once the Guide finishes, the pill turns into a small sparkle button on the card that follows it. Hover over it to see **Show thinking**, and click it to open a **Thinking** dialog that lists each thing the Guide did and what it found.

### Work with a reply

Hover over a reply from the Guide to show three buttons in its bottom right corner:

- **Copy message** copies the reply as text. Perch confirms with **Message copied to clipboard**.
- **Give positive feedback** and **Give negative feedback** mark the reply with a green thumbs up or a red thumbs down. If the Guide attached a short feedback form to that reply, clicking thumbs down opens it. Fill it in and click **Send**, or click **Cancel** to skip it.

When the Guide names a playbook in a reply, the name is a link. Clicking it opens that playbook in a new browser tab.

The **Reports** and **Pinned** buttons in the panel header narrow the conversation to the Guide's reports or to your pinned insights. See [Read reports and insights](/analyses/reports-and-insights).

## Stop the Guide

While the Guide is working, the **Send** button is replaced by a red **Stop** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Stop">
    Click the red **Stop** button at the right end of the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the conversation">
    A card reading **This message was interrupted** and **The response was stopped before it could complete.** marks the place where the Guide stopped. The field is enabled again and you can ask something else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dictate a question

Instead of typing, you can speak your question and let Perch turn it into text.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click the microphone button to the left of **Send**. Hovering over it shows **Dictate your message**. Your browser asks for permission to use the microphone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak">
    The field is replaced by a red recording dot, a waveform of your voice, and a timer that counts up to **2:00**. While Perch is getting ready, the bar reads **Starting speech recognition...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or cancel">
    Click the **Accept** button with the check mark to finish. The bar reads **Stopping transcription...** and then the text of what you said is added to the end of the field. Click the **Cancel** button with the cross to discard the recording instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and send">
    Dictation only fills the field. Read the text, fix anything that was misheard, and press **Enter** to send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Recording stops on its own after two minutes and the text is added to the field as if you had clicked **Accept**.

The microphone button is hidden while the Guide is working. If your browser blocks the microphone, Perch shows **Unable to access the microphone. Please check your settings & permissions, and try again.**

## Answer a Guide Question

Sometimes the Guide needs more detail before it can continue. When that happens, a question card takes the place of the field at the bottom of the panel. Its heading is either a title the Guide chose or **Guide Question**, and it carries a **Pending** badge. You cannot type a new message until you answer or cancel it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    If the card is collapsed, its heading also shows the first question. Click the heading to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the question">
    Each question is one of three kinds. Pick one option, tick one or more options, or type into a field that reads **Type your answer...**. Some choice questions end with **Other**, which opens a field reading **Please specify...** for your own answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move through the questions">
    When there is more than one question, a counter under the answer reads **1 of 3** or similar, with arrows on either side. Below it, **Required** in red or **Optional** tells you whether you can leave the question blank. Click **Next** to go to the next question. **Next** stays disabled until you answer a required question. You can also press **Enter**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    On the last question the button reads **Submit**. Click it to send your answers. The badge changes to **Answered**, the card moves up into the conversation, and the Guide carries on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To skip the whole card, click **Cancel**. The badge changes to **Cancelled** and the field returns. Click a card's heading at any time to collapse or expand it.

## Accept or reject a Query Suggestion

When the Guide proposes a chart, it sends a **Query Suggestion** card with a title, a **Pending** badge, and a short description. The card lists what the query uses under **Metrics**, **Dimensions**, **Filters**, **Grain**, and **Dates**. Only the parts the query needs are shown.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the suggestion">
    Check the title, the description, and the pills under each heading. When a filter has more than two values, the pill shows the first two and **+ 3 more** or similar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it goes">
    Click one of the buttons at the bottom of the card:

    - **Update step 1**, or whichever step number is shown, replaces that step's chart with the suggested query. The number is the step the Guide had in mind, or otherwise the step you have open. Perch confirms with **Proposal accepted and step updated**.
    - **Add as New Step** adds the query as a new step at the end of the analysis and opens it. Perch confirms with **Proposal accepted and new step created**.
    - **Reject** turns the suggestion down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the Guide why, if you rejected it">
    After **Reject**, a prompt reading **Why are you rejecting this query?** appears above the field. Type a reason in the field that reads **Add optional context...** and click **Send**, or click **Dismiss**. Sending any new message also closes the prompt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you decide, the badge changes to **Accepted** or **Rejected** and the card collapses. An accepted card also shows **Applied to Step 1** or the number of the step it changed. Click the card's heading to expand it again.

### Undo an update

After you click **Update step**, an **Undo** button appears next to the badge. Click it to put the step's query and settings back the way they were.

<Note>
**Undo** is available for two minutes after you accept, and only while the step still shows the suggested query. As soon as you or the Guide change that step again, the button disappears.
</Note>

## What you can do in an analysis you do not own

If you open an analysis that another member owns, the field at the bottom of the panel is disabled and a notice explains that the Guide is turned off in viewer mode. You can read the whole conversation, copy replies, and open the **Thinking** dialogs. You cannot send messages, stop the Guide, answer questions, or accept or reject suggestions.

To ask your own questions about the same data, clone the analysis from the analyses list. The clone starts with an empty conversation.
