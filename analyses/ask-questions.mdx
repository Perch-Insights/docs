---
title: "Ask the Guide a question"
description: "Type or dictate a question in the Guide panel, answer its follow-ups, and turn its query suggestions into steps."
---

After this page you can type or dictate a question in the **Guide** panel, stop the Guide, answer a **Guide Question**, and accept, reject, or undo a **Query Suggestion** so it becomes a step on the canvas.

## Ask a question

The **Guide** panel is on the right of every analysis. The field at the bottom reads **Ask about your data...**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Type in the field. Press **Shift+Enter** for a line break.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press **Enter** or click **Send**. Your question appears on the right of the conversation and the Guide's answer on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the answer">
    While the Guide works, the field is disabled and a pill names what the Guide is doing. When it finishes, click the sparkle button on the reply to open the **Thinking** dialog, which lists what the Guide did and found.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Guide uses the step you have open on the canvas as context. Select a different step before you send to ask about that one.

### Work with a reply

Hover over a reply to show three buttons in its bottom right corner: **Copy message**, **Give positive feedback**, and **Give negative feedback**. If the Guide attached a short feedback form to that reply, thumbs down opens it.

When the Guide names a playbook in a reply, the name is a link that opens the playbook in a new browser tab.

## Stop the Guide

While the Guide is working, **Send** becomes a red **Stop** button. Click it to stop the Guide. A card reading **This message was interrupted** marks where it stopped, and the field is enabled again.

## Dictate a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click the microphone button to the left of **Send**. Your browser asks for permission to use the microphone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak">
    A red recording dot, a waveform, and a timer replace the field. Recording stops on its own at **2:00**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept, edit, and send">
    Click **Accept** to add what you said to the end of the field, fix anything misheard, and press **Enter**. Click **Cancel** to discard the recording instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The microphone button is hidden while the Guide is working.

## Answer a Guide Question

When the Guide needs more detail, a question card replaces the field, headed with a title the Guide chose or **Guide Question**. You cannot send a new message until you answer or cancel it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer each question">
    Pick one option, tick one or more options, or type into the field that reads **Type your answer...**. Some choice questions end with **Other**, which opens a field reading **Please specify...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move through the questions">
    With more than one question, a counter such as **1 of 3** appears under the answer, marked **Required** or **Optional**. Click **Next** or press **Enter**. **Next** stays disabled until you answer a required question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    On the last question the button reads **Submit**. Click it to send your answers. The card moves up into the conversation and the Guide carries on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To skip the whole card, click **Cancel**.

## Accept or reject a Query Suggestion

When the Guide proposes a chart, it sends a **Query Suggestion** card with a title, a short description, and what the query uses under **Metrics**, **Dimensions**, **Filters**, **Grain**, and **Dates**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose where it goes">
    Click one of the buttons at the bottom of the card:

    - **Update step 1**, or whichever step number is shown, replaces that step's chart with the suggested query.
    - **Add as New Step** adds the query as a new step at the end of the analysis and opens it.
    - **Reject** turns the suggestion down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the Guide why, if you rejected it">
    A prompt reading **Why are you rejecting this query?** appears above the field. Type a reason and click **Send**, or click **Dismiss**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Undo an update

After **Update step**, an **Undo** button appears on the card for two minutes. Click it to put the step's query and settings back the way they were. The button disappears as soon as you or the Guide change that step again.

## Good to know

- Long questions are cut off after a few lines. Click **Show more** to read the whole thing.
- In an analysis another member owns, the field is disabled and the panel shows **The AI analyst is disabled in viewer mode.** To ask your own questions, clone the analysis. See [What a viewer can do](/sharing/share-an-analysis#what-a-viewer-can-do).
