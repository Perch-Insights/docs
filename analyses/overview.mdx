After this page you will know what an analysis is, how the Guide works with you inside it, and how each accepted query becomes a step.

## What an analysis is

An analysis is a conversation with the Guide and a set of charts, side by side. It takes you from "something changed" to "here is why it changed and what to do next." The **Analyses** screen lists all saved analyses in your workspace. Any member can start one with **New Analysis**.

## What the Guide does

The Guide is Perch's AI analyst. It works in the **Guide** panel on the right of every analysis. It interprets your data, explains patterns and drivers, and suggests the next step. It finds out why performance changed before it recommends anything.

When the Guide needs more detail, it asks you a **Guide Question**. When it has a chart to propose, it sends a **Query Suggestion**. You decide what happens to each suggestion.

## Steps

Each accepted query becomes a step in the analysis. A step is one chart, built from metrics, dimensions, filters, a grain, and dates. The **Analysis Steps** panel lists the steps by number. A step can have sub-steps, numbered one level down, such as 4.1 and 4.2. Sub-steps never nest further.

The Guide uses the step you have open as its context. Open a different step to ask about that one.

## Reports

When the Guide has an answer, it writes an **Insight report** into the conversation. Each section of the report is an insight, shown as its own card. Reports carry no step number. A finished report does not change. If you ask for changes, the Guide writes a new one.

## How analyses relate to the rest of Perch

- A playbook saves a line of analysis so you can run it again on fresh data. Each run is its own analysis. See [Playbooks](/playbooks/overview).
- Every analysis you start is private. Make it public so other members can read and clone it. See [Reports and sharing](/sharing/overview).
- Dashboards and alerts tell you where to look. An analysis is where you find out why.

## What you can and cannot change

In an analysis you own, you ask the Guide questions, accept or reject its suggestions, and rename or delete the analysis. In an analysis another member owns, you are a viewer. You can read it, but you cannot ask the Guide anything until you clone it.

Neither you nor the Guide can change metric definitions, dashboards, or journeys from an analysis. The Perch team manages those. See [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).
