title: "Manage steps"
description: "Move between the steps of an analysis, and add, copy, duplicate, reorder, and delete them."
---

On this page you learn to move between the steps of an analysis and to add, copy, duplicate, and delete steps and sub-steps.

Open the **Analysis Steps** panel with **View All Steps** at the top left of the canvas. Use **Go to previous step** and **Go to next step** to move one step at a time.
