title: "Build a step"
description: "Choose the metrics, dimensions, and filters a step shows."
---

On this page you learn to choose what a step shows with the **Metrics**, **Dimensions**, and **Filters** pickers above the data.

The pickers sit in the row below the step title. Each shows a count of what is selected. Next to them are the granularity and the time period, covered in [Set the time period](/analyses/set-the-time-period).

Your changes are not applied while you pick. When you close a picker, the data is blurred and two buttons appear. Click **Update Analysis** to run the step with your changes. Click **Cancel** to go back, and then **Discard all Changes** to confirm.

## Choose metrics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the picker">
    Click **Metrics**. The list of metrics in your workspace is on the left. **Selected Metrics** is on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a metric">
    Type in **Search for metrics...** to narrow the list by name. Each metric shows a green label for the area of the business it comes from. Hover over the label to read what that area covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the definition">
    Hover over a metric and click its info icon. A dialog shows the **Description** and the **Formula**. Click the open icon next to the metric name to open its entry in the [metrics catalog](/metrics-catalog/look-up-a-metric).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add, hide, or remove">
    Click a metric to add it to **Selected Metrics**. Drag selected metrics to change their order. Click the eye icon to hide a metric from the chart and table without removing it. Click the X to remove it. **Clear** removes all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the picker, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A step needs at least one metric. With none selected, the canvas shows **Select at least 1 metric to update the query** and you cannot update.

## Break down by dimensions

A dimension splits each metric into one row or line per value, such as one per agent or per channel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the picker">
    Click **Dimensions**. Type in **Search for dimensions...** or scroll the list. Each dimension has the same green label and info icon as a metric.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and order">
    Click a dimension to add it to **Selected Dimensions**. Drag to change the order. Click the X to remove one, or **Clear** to remove all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the picker, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter the data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the picker">
    Click **Filters**. The left side lists dimensions. Dimensions you already break down by appear first under **Suggested Dimensions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a dimension">
    Click a dimension to see its values on the right. Type in **Search for values...** to find a value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose values">
    Tick the values to keep. **Select all** ticks every value. **Clear** unticks them. The dimension shows how many values it filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more filters">
    Pick another dimension and choose its values. Each dimension you filter adds to the others. **Clear** above the dimension list removes every filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click outside the picker, then click **Update Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- You can filter on the values of a dimension only. You cannot filter on the value of a metric, such as only rows above a threshold.
- The Perch team sets up and maintains the metrics and dimensions in your workspace. You cannot add or edit them. See [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).
- If you are not the owner of the analysis, you can change a step to explore, but your changes are not saved to the analysis.
- Until you rename a step, its title updates to describe the new data each time you update it.
