After this page you will be able to read an **Insight report** card in an analysis, tell the three insight types apart, copy or vote on an insight or the whole report, download the report as a file, and narrow the conversation to just its reports.

## Read a report

The Guide writes the result of an analysis up as an insight report. It appears as a card in the **Guide** panel, at the point in the conversation where the Guide produced it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the card">
    Look for a card with a document icon whose header reads **Insight report** above the report's title.

    <Frame caption="An expanded Insight report card with its summary and first insight">
      <img src="/images/reports-and-insights/find-the-card.png" alt="Card with a document icon, the label Insight report, the title Inbound Calls and Queue Time May to July 2026, a 1 pinned count, a one-sentence summary, and below it an insight card titled Inbound Call Volume Trend with Tracking and Pinned badges, a Context paragraph, and a Key Findings table of monthly call totals" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the Guide to finish">
    While the Guide is still writing, the header reads **Writing…** next to a spinner. Insights appear one at a time. The summary and the buttons at the bottom of the card appear once the report is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary and the insights">
    The body starts with a one-sentence summary. Below it, each insight is its own card with a title, a type badge, and the insight text. An insight you have pinned also carries a **Pinned** badge and a colored bar down its left edge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the card's header to collapse it to the title line, and again to expand it.

## Tell the insight types apart

Each insight carries one of three badges:

- **Monitoring** insights, with an eye icon, report the recurring health of a metric or how a rate is moving.
- **Tracking** insights, with a dashboard icon, follow items over time against a target.
- **Investigation** insights, with a document icon, dig into the root cause of something that changed.

## Copy or vote on an insight

Every insight card has a row of buttons along its bottom edge. Hover over a button to see its name.

- **Copy insight** copies the insight's title and text.
- **Give positive feedback** and **Give negative feedback** mark the insight with a green thumbs up or a red thumbs down. Clicking the other thumb replaces your vote.
- **Pin insight**, at the right end of the row, appears only on a finished report in an analysis you own. See [Pin insights](/sharing/pin-insights).

## Copy, vote on, or download a whole report

Once the report is complete, a second row of buttons appears at the bottom right of the report card.

- **Copy report** copies the whole report as text, including the title and every insight.
- **Give positive feedback** and **Give negative feedback** vote on the report. Voting on the report does not vote on any of its insights, and the other way round.
- **Download report** opens a menu of file formats.

### Download a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the download menu">
    Click **Download report** at the right end of the button row. It stays disabled until the first file is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a format">
    The menu lists **Markdown**, **Plain Text**, **Word Document**, and **PDF Document**. Perch prepares each file separately, so a format is greyed out until its file is ready.

    <Frame caption="The download menu open below the report's button row">
      <img src="/images/reports-and-insights/pick-a-format.png" alt="A row of four icon buttons for copy, thumbs up, thumbs down, and download, with the download button highlighted and a menu below it listing Markdown, Plain Text, Word Document, and PDF Document" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    Click a format. The file opens in a new browser tab and downloads as `insight-report` with that format's extension, such as `insight-report.pdf`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See only the reports in an analysis

The **Reports** button in the **Guide** panel header shows how many reports the conversation contains.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Reports">
    Click **Reports** in the panel header. The heading **Reports in this conversation** appears above the list.

    <Frame caption="The Guide panel filtered to its reports">
      <img src="/images/reports-and-insights/click-reports.png" alt="Panel header reading Guide with a pressed Reports button showing a count of 1 and a Pinned button showing 1, the heading Reports in this conversation with a Show full conversation link on the right, and one collapsed Insight report card titled Inbound Calls and Queue Time May to July 2026" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the report you want">
    In this view every report starts collapsed, so you see one title line per report. Click a header to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the full conversation">
    Click **Show full conversation** next to the heading, or click **Reports** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- A finished report does not change. If you ask the Guide to change a report, it writes a new report as a new card, and the earlier one stays as it was.
- Sending a new message, or answering or cancelling a **Guide Question**, returns the panel from the **Reports** view to the full conversation.
- The **Pinned** button next to **Reports** works the same way for pinned insights. See [Pin insights](/sharing/pin-insights).
- In an analysis you do not own, you can read, copy, and download every report, but the report's vote buttons and **Pin insight** are hidden. The vote buttons on each insight still appear, but Perch keeps votes only from the owner. See [What a viewer can do](/sharing/share-an-analysis#what-a-viewer-can-do).
