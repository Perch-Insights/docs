After this page you will be able to recognize an **Insight report** card in an analysis, expand it to read the summary and each insight, tell **Monitoring**, **Tracking**, and **Investigation** insights apart, copy or vote on an insight or the whole report, download the report as a Markdown, plain text, Word, or PDF file, and narrow the conversation to just its reports.

## Read a report

When the Guide runs an analysis for you, it writes the result up as an insight report. The report appears as a card in the **Guide** panel, in the place in the conversation where the Guide produced it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the card">
    Look for a card with a document icon whose header reads **Insight report** above the report's title. The header is grey and the card is open by default, so the body is already visible below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the Guide to finish">
    While the Guide is still writing, the header reads **Writing…** next to a spinner and the bottom of the card reads **Writing the report...**. Insights appear one at a time as the Guide finishes each one. The summary and the buttons at the bottom of the card appear once the report is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary and the insights">
    The body starts with a one-sentence summary of the whole report. Below it, each insight is its own card with a title, a type badge, and the insight text. An insight you have pinned also carries a **Pinned** badge and a colored bar down its left edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collapse it when you are done">
    Click the card's header to collapse it to the title line. Click the header again to expand it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A finished report does not change. If you ask the Guide to change a report, the Guide writes a new report as a new card, and the earlier one stays in the conversation as it was.

## Tell the insight types apart

Each insight carries one of three badges that tells you what kind of finding it is:

- **Monitoring** insights, with an eye icon, report the recurring health of a metric or how a rate is moving.
- **Tracking** insights, with a dashboard icon, follow items over time against a target.
- **Investigation** insights, with a document icon, dig into the root cause of something that changed.

## Copy or vote on an insight

Every insight card has a row of buttons along its bottom edge. Hover over a button to see its name.

- **Copy insight** copies the insight's title and text. Perch confirms with **Insight copied to clipboard**.
- **Give positive feedback** and **Give negative feedback** mark the insight with a green thumbs up or a red thumbs down. Clicking the other thumb replaces your vote.
- **Pin insight** at the right end of the row keeps the insight in front of the Guide for the rest of the analysis. It appears only on a finished report in an analysis you own. See [Pin insights](/sharing/pin-insights).

## Copy, vote on, or download a whole report

Once the report is complete, a row of buttons appears at the bottom right of the report card, below the last insight. Hover over a button to see its name.

- **Copy report** copies the whole report as text, including the title and every insight. Perch confirms with **Message copied to clipboard**.
- **Give positive feedback** and **Give negative feedback** mark the report with a green thumbs up or a red thumbs down. These two buttons appear only in an analysis you own. Voting on the report does not vote on any of its insights, and the other way round.
- **Download report** opens a menu of file formats.

### Download a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the download menu">
    Click **Download report** at the right end of the button row. The button stays disabled until the first file is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a format">
    The menu lists **Markdown**, **Plain Text**, **Word Document**, and **PDF Document**. Perch prepares each file separately after the report is finished, so a format that is not ready yet is greyed out until its file is finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    Click a format. The file downloads through a new browser tab with the name `insight-report` and the extension for that format, such as `insight-report.pdf`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See only the reports in an analysis

The **Reports** button in the **Guide** panel header shows how many reports the conversation contains. Use it to skip past the questions and replies.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Reports">
    Click **Reports** in the panel header. Hovering over it first shows **Filter to reports**. The button turns solid and the heading **Reports in this conversation** appears above the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the report you want">
    In this view every report card starts collapsed, so you see one title line per report. Click a header to expand that report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the full conversation">
    Click **Show full conversation** next to the heading, or click **Reports** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the conversation has no reports yet, the list reads **No reports yet. Run an analysis and it will show up here.** While the Guide is working, it reads **Working on it — new reports will appear here.** instead.

<Note>
Sending a new message, or answering or cancelling a **Guide Question**, returns the panel to the full conversation.
</Note>

The **Pinned** button next to **Reports** works the same way for pinned insights. See [Pin insights](/sharing/pin-insights).

The **Reports** column in the analyses list shows the same count for each analysis. See [Start an analysis](/analyses/start-an-analysis).

## What you can do in an analysis you do not own

In an analysis another member owns, you can read, expand, and collapse every report, copy a report or an insight, and download the report in any format. The thumbs up and thumbs down buttons on the report are hidden, and there is no **Pin insight** button.

<Warning>
The thumbs up and thumbs down buttons on each insight still appear in an analysis you do not own, but Perch saves votes only from the owner. A vote you give there is not kept.
</Warning>
