title: "Read reports and insights"
description: "Read an insight report the Guide writes, review each insight, give feedback, and download the report."

After this page you will be able to recognize an **Insight report** card in an analysis, expand it to read the summary and each insight, tell monitoring, tracking, and investigation insights apart, copy or vote on an insight or the whole report, and download the report as Markdown, plain text, a Word document, or a PDF.
