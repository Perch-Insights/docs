---
title: "Start an analysis"
description: "Open a new analysis from the analyses list and find the ones you already have."
---

After this page you will be able to open the **Analyses** list for your workspace, click **New Analysis** to start an analysis with the Guide, rename the analysis, mark it as a favorite, and find an existing analysis by name, owner, or favorite status.

## Open the analyses list

The analyses list is the page you land on after you sign in. To get back to it from anywhere in a workspace, click the **Analyses** icon in the left sidebar. It is the first icon under the workspace button, and hovering over it shows the label **Analyses**.

The page has an **Analyses** section described as **All saved analyses in this workspace.** Next to the heading, a count tells you how many analyses are shown out of the total. If the workspace has any playbooks, a **Playbooks** section appears above it, described as **Reusable analyses you can re-run.**

The list shows every analysis in the workspace that is not private, plus your own private ones. Each row shows:

- **Name**, with a star button in front of it for marking a favorite. Click the name to open the analysis.
- **Owner**, which reads **You** for analyses you started.
- **Reports**, the number of reports the analysis contains.
- **Created At**, the date and time the analysis was started.
- **Last Modified**, shown as how long ago the analysis last changed.
- **Playbook**, which names the playbook the analysis came from, if any. Click it to open that playbook.

Each row also has a **Clone Analysis** button, and rows you own have a **Delete Analysis** button.

If the workspace has no analyses yet, the list says **No analyses found** and **Create an analysis to get started**.

## Start a new analysis

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Analysis">
    On the analyses list, click **New Analysis** at the right of the **Analyses** heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the analysis to open">
    Perch creates an analysis named **Untitled** and opens it. The name is the title at the top left of the page. Under it, **by** is followed by the owner badge, which reads **You**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way around">
    The middle of the page is the canvas. It shows the first step of the analysis, titled **Initial Analysis**, with the step number in a button at the top left. The panel on the right is headed **Guide**. This is where you ask questions. See [Ask questions](/analyses/ask-questions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Analysis names are unique. If an analysis named **Untitled** already exists, Perch names the new one **Untitled** followed by today's date, and adds a counter in parentheses if that name is taken too.

To go back to the list from inside an analysis, click the home button at the top left of the header. Hovering over it shows **All Analyses**.

<Note>
You can also start an analysis without leaving the one you are in. Click the menu button at the top right of the header and choose **New Analysis**. The new analysis opens in a new browser tab, so the one you were working in stays open.
</Note>

You can collapse the **Guide** panel to give the canvas more room. Click the arrow button on the left edge of the panel, labelled **Collapse panel**. Click it again, now labelled **Expand panel**, to bring the panel back. You can also drag the left edge of the panel to change its width.

## Rename an analysis

Only the owner of an analysis can rename it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the title">
    Click the analysis title at the top left of the header. It turns into a text field holding the current name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name">
    Type the new name. A name can be up to 255 characters long. When you get close to the limit, a counter appears under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Enter** or click anywhere outside the field. Perch confirms with the message **Title updated successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Perch trims extra spaces from the name before saving it. If you clear the name completely, Perch keeps the old one and shows **Title must have at least one character.** If another analysis in the workspace already has that name, ignoring capitalization, Perch keeps the old one and shows **This title is already in use. Please choose another.**

If you are not the owner, the title is not clickable and a **Viewer** badge appears at the top right of the header.

## Mark an analysis as a favorite

Favorites are personal. Marking an analysis as a favorite does not change what other members see.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the star">
    Inside an analysis, click the star button at the top right of the header. Hovering over it shows **Add to Favorites**. Perch confirms with **Added to Favorites** and the star turns yellow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the favorite">
    Click the star again. Hovering over it now shows **Remove from Favorites**, and Perch confirms with **Removed from Favorites**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can do the same from the analyses list by clicking the star at the start of a row. There the confirmation reads **Analysis status updated**.

Favorites always appear at the top of the analyses list, ahead of everything else.

## Find an existing analysis

The row under the **Analyses** heading is labelled **Filters:** and holds three controls. Combine them as you like.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by favorite status">
    Click the first filter, marked with a star and reading **All**. Choose **Favorites** to see only your favorites, or **No Favorites** to hide them. Choose **All** to show everything again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by owner">
    Click the second filter, reading **All owners**. Pick one or more members from the list. You appear at the top as **You**. The filter shows the member's name when you pick one and **2 selected** or similar when you pick more. Pick **Unknown** to find analyses whose owner is no longer a known member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by name">
    Type part of a name in the field that reads **Search...**. The list updates as you type and matches names that are close to what you typed, not only exact matches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list is ordered by **Last Modified** with the newest first. Click the **Created At** or **Last Modified** heading to sort by that column, and click it again to reverse the order. The list is paged. The page controls sit at the right end of the filter row.

<Note>
While you have text in the search field, results are ordered by how closely they match your text, and the **Created At** and **Last Modified** headings no longer sort. Clear the search field to sort by date again.
</Note>
