On this page you learn to start an analysis with the Guide, rename it, mark it as a favorite, find it again, and delete it.

## Open the analyses list

You land on the analyses list after you sign in. To get back to it, click the **Analyses** icon in the left sidebar, the first icon under the workspace button. From inside an analysis, you can also click the home button at the top left of the header, labelled **All Analyses**.

The **Analyses** section lists every analysis in the workspace that is not private, plus your own private ones. If the workspace has any playbooks, a **Playbooks** section appears above it.

Each row shows the analysis **Name**, its **Owner**, which reads **You** for your own, the number of **Reports** it contains, **Created At**, **Last Modified**, and the **Playbook** it came from, if any. Click the name to open the analysis, or the playbook name to open that playbook. Each row also has a **Clone Analysis** button, and rows you own have a **Delete Analysis** button.

## Start a new analysis

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Analysis">
    On the analyses list, click **New Analysis** at the right of the **Analyses** heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the analysis to open">
    Perch creates an analysis named **Untitled** and opens it. The name is the title at the top left of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way around">
    The middle of the page is the canvas, showing the first step, titled **Initial Analysis**. The panel on the right is headed **Guide**. See [Ask questions](/analyses/ask-questions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename an analysis

Only the owner can rename an analysis. See [What a viewer can do](/sharing/share-an-analysis#what-a-viewer-can-do).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the title">
    Click the analysis title at the top left of the header. It turns into a text field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name">
    A name can be up to 255 characters. It cannot be empty, and no other analysis in the workspace can have the same name, ignoring capitalization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Enter** or click outside the field. If the name is empty or already in use, Perch keeps the old name and tells you why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark an analysis as a favorite

Favorites are personal and always appear at the top of your analyses list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the star">
    Inside an analysis, click the star at the top right of the header, labelled **Add to Favorites**. On the analyses list, click the star at the start of the row instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the star again to remove the favorite">
    The star is yellow while the analysis is a favorite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find an existing analysis

The row under the **Analyses** heading is labelled **Filters:**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by favorite status">
    Click the first filter, marked with a star and reading **All**. Choose **Favorites** or **No Favorites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by owner">
    Click **All owners** and pick one or more members. You appear at the top as **You**. Pick **Unknown** to find analyses whose owner is no longer a member of the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by name">
    Type part of a name in the **Search...** field. The list updates as you type and includes close matches, not only exact ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list shows the most recently modified analyses first. Click the **Created At** or **Last Modified** heading to sort by that column, and click it again to reverse the order.

<Note>
While the search field has text, results are ordered by how closely they match, and the date headings do not sort. Clear the field to sort by date again.
</Note>

## Delete an analysis

Only the owner can delete an analysis.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Delete Analysis">
    In an open analysis, click the menu button at the top right of the header and choose **Delete Analysis**. On the analyses list, click the trash button at the right end of the row instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    A dialog titled **Delete Analysis** opens. Click **Delete**, or **Cancel** to keep the analysis. If you were inside the analysis, Perch returns you to the analyses list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Deleting cannot be undone. [Clone the analysis](/sharing/share-an-analysis#clone-an-analysis) first if you want to keep a copy.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- Analysis names are unique. If an analysis named **Untitled** already exists, the new one gets today's date added to its name.
- The menu at the top right of the header also has **New Analysis**. It opens a new analysis in a new browser tab.
- To give the canvas more room, click **Collapse panel** on the left edge of the **Guide** panel. Click **Expand panel** to bring it back.
