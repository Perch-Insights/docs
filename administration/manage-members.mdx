title: "Manage members"

After this page you will be able to add and remove members, organize them into groups, and choose which dashboards each group sees.

<Note>
You need to be allowed to manage members. If you do not see **Settings** in the sidebar, or a settings screen shows **NOT AUTHORIZED**, ask one of your administrators. See [Administration](/administration/overview).
</Note>

## Review your members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the organization settings">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar. The **Users** screen opens in the **Organization** panel, with **Showing N users** under the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the list">
    Each row shows the member's **Name** and email, and their **Groups**. Click a group name to open that group in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Click **Groups** and check one or more groups to show only their members. Type in **Search...** to find a member. The list shows 20 members per page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add or change a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a member">
    Click **Add user**. In the **New user** form, enter **Email**, **First name**, and **Last name**. Choose groups in **Groups (optional)**, then click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update a member">
    Open the row's actions menu and click **Update**. You can change the name and groups, but not the email. Click **Update** to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend an invite">
    If a member has not confirmed their account yet, their actions menu also has **Resend Invite**. Click it and you see **Invite has been sent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a member">
    Click **Delete** in the row's actions menu. Type the member's first and last name to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organize members into groups

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the groups list">
    Click **Groups** in the **Organization** panel. Each group shows its **Name** and how many **Users** it has.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a group">
    Click **Add Group**. Enter a **Name** in the **New Group** form and click **Create**. To rename or remove a group later, use **Update group** or **Delete group** in its actions menu. Deleting asks you to type the group's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members to a group">
    Click a group's name. Its **Members** tab opens. Click **Add member**, then **Select users**. Start typing names, pick the members, and click **Add members**. To invite someone new straight into the group, choose **Add new user** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a member from a group">
    Click **Remove** on the member's row and type their name to confirm. The member stays in your organization. They only leave this group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the dashboards a group sees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the group's dashboards">
    On the group's screen, click the **Dashboards** tab. The header shows how many dashboards are selected out of the total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn dashboards on or off">
    Turn the switch next to a dashboard's **Name** to add it to the group. Turn it off to remove it. You see **Dashboard added to the group** or **Dashboard removed from the group**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you are done, click the cross at the top right to go back to where you were.

## Good to know

- The organization settings cover your whole organization, not one workspace.
- Every member of a group sees the group's dashboards in their **Dashboards** panel. Two members of the same workspace can see different dashboards.
- The Perch team builds dashboards and alerts during onboarding. You choose which groups see a dashboard, not what it shows. To ask for a change, click **Support** in the sidebar.
- If you do not see **Remove** on a group's members, you are not allowed to remove members from groups.
