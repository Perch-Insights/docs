After this page you will know who sees the organization settings, what they cover, and which changes belong to the Perch team instead.

## Who administers Perch

A few members in your organization look after access for everyone else. They see **Settings** in the sidebar. Most members do not, because it appears only for members allowed to manage members or workspaces. If you do not see **Settings**, ask one of your administrators for any change on this page.

The organization settings cover your whole organization, not a single workspace.

## What the organization settings cover

**Settings** opens the **Organization** panel. It has up to three parts.

- **Users**: every member of your organization, with the groups each belongs to. Administrators add members, update their details, and remove them. A member who has not confirmed their account yet can be sent their invite again.
- **Groups**: named sets of members. A group decides two things: who is in it, on its **Members** tab, and which dashboards they can see, on its **Dashboards** tab.
- **Workspaces**: shown only to members allowed to create, update, or delete workspaces. It lists the workspaces in your organization.

Groups are how access works in Perch. You do not give a dashboard to one member. You add the dashboard to a group, and every member of that group sees it in their **Dashboards** panel. That is why two members of the same workspace can see different dashboards. Alerts reach members the same way: each alert notifies groups, not people.

## How administration relates to the rest of Perch

Administration decides who can reach what. It does not change what anything means.

- **Analyses and playbooks** belong to the members who create them. Each member controls whether their own work is private or public. See [Reports and sharing](/sharing/overview).
- **Dashboards** are built by the Perch team. Administrators choose which groups see them. See [Dashboards](/dashboards/overview).
- **Alerts** are configured by the Perch team, including which groups they notify. See [Alerts](/alerts/overview).
- **Metrics and dimensions** are defined in the semantic layer, which the Perch team maintains. See [Metrics catalog](/metrics-catalog/overview).

## What you can and cannot change

As an administrator, you change who has access: members, groups, and the dashboards each group sees.

You do not change metric definitions, how a dashboard is built, or when an alert fires. Those are managed services. Some changes are kept out of self-service on purpose, so your numbers stay consistent and comparable across time and teams. To ask for one, click **Support** in the sidebar. See [Self-service and managed services](/reference/self-service-and-managed-services).
